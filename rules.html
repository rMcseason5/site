01 · Race night procedure

  1. Server join. Connect to the official RMC server at least 15 minutes before the scheduled briefing time.
  2. Briefing. Attend the voice briefing on Discord. Grid order, weather, and any last-minute notes are announced here.
  3. Formation & grid. Line up in qualifying order. No overtaking before the green flag.
  4. Race start. Standing or rolling start as announced per round. Jump-starts are reviewed post-race.

02 · Sporting code

  • Driving standard. Predictable, respectful racing. Give racing room on the inside of a corner when overlapped.
  • Incidents. Contact that ends another driver's race is reviewed by race control regardless of intent.
  • Penalties. Time penalties, grid drops, or points deductions may apply depending on severity and history.
  • Blue flags. Lapped cars must yield to the leaders without unnecessarily blocking the racing line.

03 · Points & championship

  • Points are awarded down to 30th place, combining the race result with the qualifying result for that round.
  • Championship position is decided by total points across all seven rounds.
  • Ties are broken by most race wins, then by most pole positions, then most second-place finishes.
  • The Teams' Championship is the sum of every driver's points for that team, round by round — every driver on the roster contributes.
  • Results are posted on the Results page shortly after each round.

04 · Eligibility & technical requirements

  • rFactor 1, fully updated, with the current season's content pack installed.
  • A stable connection capable of maintaining the server's ping requirements.
  • Voice chat access on Discord for briefings and race control communication.

05 · Getting help

Content downloads, setup issues, or questions about a specific rule — the fastest way to get an answer is the RMC Discord ↗, before race night rather than during it.
